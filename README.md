# Openfront Senior Care

The open-source alternative to PointClickCare.

Openfront Senior Care runs a senior home on one system: residents and census, care plans and assessments, medications, incidents, staffing, billing and resident trust accounts, the home's public website with tours and availability, and the family portal. Staff can also describe a job in plain words and have it built into a workflow from tested steps.

It covers skilled nursing and long-term care, assisted living and memory care. Hospice is planned as a later module.

Built on Next.js and KeystoneJS 6 with PostgreSQL.

## Status

The foundation is being built. The data model and build plan are in progress.
