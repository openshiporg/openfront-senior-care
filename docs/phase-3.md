# Phase 3: private pay, deposits, statements, resident trust, Medicaid liability

Builds on Phase 2 (commit `38a4912`). No timer, job, cron, outbox, autopay runner or sent message exists. Month-end close and billing derivation are idempotent staff buttons per month. The 30-day conveyance, Medicaid information-request and appeal deadlines are worked out when a page loads. Rate-change notices are printable pages with a recorded delivery date; the app sends nothing.

## What shipped
- **Rates** (`billing/rates.ts`): care levels, rate schedules and lines. Publishing is a maker-checker approval (`rate.publish`, canApproveRates); lines freeze at publication. `rateChangeNotices` lists the residents who need notice and by when.
- **Derivation** (`billing/derive.ts`): a pure function of census days, coverage, care levels, published rates and Medicaid liabilities. Same input gives the same lines, order and hashes. Amounts are integer cents; rates and quantities use `decimal.js`; proration is allocated so the pieces add up to the month.
- **Month-end** (`billing/period.ts`): `billing.derive` brings the open month in line with the census; `billing.close` freezes the charges and issues one statement per resident; `billing.adjust_closed` posts the difference caused by a later change as an adjustment line in the next open month. A changed charge is superseded by a new row, a removed one is voided, nothing is edited. `verifyPeriod` re-derives a closed month and compares hashes.
- **Payments** (`billing/payments.ts`, `integrations/payment`): hand payments, deposits, refunds (second approver over the threshold; a refund never reopens a paid statement) and Stripe test card/ACH. A second charge while one is pending or unknown is refused, in the service and by a partial unique index. "Check status" only retrieves. A decline is final for its key. An idempotency error is recorded as unknown. Webhooks are verified, deduplicated by provider event id and applied once (`app/api/payments/webhook`).
- **Autopay**: a stored token charged by the "Charge stored cards" button after the close; never retried automatically.
- **Resident trust** (`trust/ledger.ts`, `trust/service.ts`): double-entry lines in BigInt, append-only. Withdrawals over `trustApprovalThresholdMinor` wait for a second signer, never the preparer. A reversal is a new transaction. Closing a ledger starts the 30-day conveyance clock. The three-way reconciliation (bank, bank-control book, sum of resident ledgers) is prepared by one person and signed by another.
- **Medicaid liability** (`billing/medicaid.ts`): monthly patient liability, correction by a new row, information-request and appeal clocks.
- **Statement PDF**: made on request at `/api/statements/<id>/pdf` (`billing/statementPdf.ts`), through the signed-in session's list access.
- **Family portal APIs only**: `portalStatements` and `portalTrustBalance`, behind the `statements` and `trust_balance` grants and two-step sign-in, each read audited. No portal screen.
- **Screens**: Billing (months and statements), Statement, Rates and notices, Medicaid liability, Resident funds, Resident ledger. Navigation through `navigation.ts`; every list is facility-scoped and money and PHI reads are gated by role.
- **Seed** (`seed/billing.ts`, onboarding `loadBillingSetup`): care levels, published assisted-living and memory-care schedules, a charge list and the resident funds account. Every figure is invented.
- **Database guards** (`20261112110000_phase_3_billing_trust_guards`): one live charge per key, one in-flight card/ACH payment per statement, append-only triggers (trust lines, payment allocations and events), a charge guard, a deferred trust-balanced constraint trigger and the preparer-is-not-signer check. `20261112120000_phase_3_compound_uniques` adds the four compound uniques declared in `prismaConstraints.ts`. All migrations are additive: no ALTER, UPDATE or DROP of an existing table.

## Open items (verify, built generically)
- Rate, notice and conveyance day counts follow the generic defaults (30/10/90 days); Ohio specifics are not asserted.
- The trust account is one pooled account per community; multi-account banks are not modelled.
- Card and ACH run against Stripe test mode only.

## r1 fixes (review items M1–M6, M8)

- **M1 resident credit.** A settle that finds nothing left to allocate records the excess as a `ResidentCredit` (open, with the amount left). It shows on the statement screen under "Credit on account" and can be applied to a statement (`credit.apply`, a `credit` payment) or refunded (`refund.request` with `creditId`). Never silent.
- **M2 deposit rules.** The Ohio pack carries `depositRefund` per deposit kind (refundable, refundWindowDays from receipt, forfeitable). A refund outside the rule is refused. `deposit.forfeit` (held → forfeited) needs a second approver. `community_fee` is a deposit kind.
- **M3 second person.** The census gives occupancy: the first occupant of an apartment that day pays rent, the second pays the `second_person` rate.
- **M4 care level.** An increase bills only from the later of its effective date and `noticeDeliveredOn` + the pack's `careLevelIncreaseNoticeDays`; a decrease applies at once. `care_level.assign` creates the row from AL points, or by hand with a reason, and supersedes the current one.
- **M5 rate shape.** `per:'day'` and the `daily` method give rate × days. `rate.add_line` refuses combinations that make no sense (`checkRateShape`).
- **M6 adjustments and write-offs.** `adjustment.create` and `writeoff.create` write a `BalanceAdjustment`. At or above `writeOffApprovalThresholdMinor` they wait for a different person with `canApproveWriteOffs`. They are append-only; a correction supersedes. `Statement.adjustedMinor` carries the total, and the balance is total − paid − adjusted.
- **M8 database guards** (`20261112150000_phase_3_r1_money_guards`): triggers in the `hs_money_period` pattern for Statement, BillingPeriod, RateSchedule, RateLine, Charge, Payment, Refund, Deposit, MedicaidLiability, CareLevelAssignment, BalanceAdjustment, ResidentCredit, TrustTransaction and TrustReconciliation, plus a never-negative constraint trigger on the trust ledger. `scripts/sqlguards.mjs` proves each probe is refused as a non-owner role.

## Phase 3b (review item M7)

Built on the same rules: BigInt minor units, every write a `billingCommand` op, every clock and alert worked out on read, every operation a staff button, corrections by reversal.

- **Payment plans** (`billing/plans.ts`, `billing/ar.ts`; Statement page). A plan splits an issued statement's open balance into 2–24 monthly instalments that sum to it exactly (largest remainder, earlier instalments carry the extra cents). Each instalment is charged by **Charge** through the ordinary card/ACH path (`recordIntent` locks the Statement row, so one open card payment per statement still holds, and the intent commits before the processor is called). A **declined instalment is never charged again automatically**: Charge refuses it, autopay skips any statement on a plan (`on_plan`), the portal refuses it, and only the separate **Retry** op (`plan.retry_instalment`, canManagePayments) charges it again. A one-off charge on a statement with an active plan is refused. A plan is cancelled, never edited; cancelling is refused while an instalment has a payment in flight.
- **Collections ageing** (Billing page): current / 1–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 / over 90 by days past due, from issued statements net of payments and adjustments (the M6 balance), computed when the page loads. A statement on a plan is still aged, and marked.
- **GL export** (Billing page → `GET /api/billing/gl-export?communityId&month`): a balanced journal (charges by kind and responsibility, payments by method and day, adjustments and write-offs) as CSV with exact decimal text and formula-neutralised cells. Needs canReadBilling plus canManageBilling or canManagePayments and the community assignment; each export writes an audit event.
- **Medicaid applications** (`billing/medicaidApplication.ts`; Medicaid page): preparing → submitted → info_requested → submitted/appealed, approved, denied → appealed → approved/denied. The reply clock (the letter's date or 10 days) and the 90-day appeal window are computed on read; an appeal past its window is refused. **Pending AR** is the resident's open balance on statements from the requested month on, while undecided. One open application per stay (partial unique index).
- **Patient liability schedule**: effective-dated monthly liability and personal needs allowance. A new determination end-dates the earlier row (or supersedes it if it starts the same day); amounts are never edited (trigger). The month's derivation uses the schedule in effect (`scheduleForMonth`, prorated by Medicaid days as before); an explicit monthly MedicaidLiability row still overrides it for its month.
- **PNA tracking** (Medicaid page): this month's allowance from the schedule against posted trust deposits for the resident this month: credited, short by $X, or no schedule. Read only; there is no new trust transaction kind.
- **Trust authorisation** (`trust/more.ts`, `trust/authorisation.ts`; ledger page). A withdrawal needs the ledger's active written authorisation covering withdrawals, within its per-withdrawal limit (`no_authorisation`, `not_authorised`, `over_authorised_limit`); conveyance after discharge or death does not. An authorisation with no limit, or a limit over the pack's `trustAuthorisationApproval.overMinor` (US-OH: $500), waits for a second person with canApproveTrust through Approvals (`trust.authorise`, applied on approve, never the recorder). One active per ledger. The database refuses activating one without a different approver, and its terms are never edited.
- **Interest allocation** (`trust.allocate_interest`, Trust page): one month's bank interest shared by average daily balance (largest remainder, ties to the earlier ledger) and posted as one balanced `interest` transaction per share, dated the month's last day. Only after the month ends; once per account and month (unique + frozen run row; a deferred trigger checks the run's posted shares add up to its interest).
- **Quarterly trust statements** (`trust.quarterly_statements` button; PDF at `/api/trust/statements/[id]/pdf`): opening, each entry with running balance, money in and out, closing; made once per ledger and quarter after the quarter ends, frozen.
- **SSI/Medicaid resource alert** (Trust page): a Medicaid resident (active Medicaid FFS or MCO coverage) whose funds reach the pack's limit less the margin is near, above the limit is over (US-OH pack: $2,000 limit, alert at $1,800). Computed on read.
- **Surety bond** (`trust.record_bond`, canApproveTrust): carrier, number, amount, dates; a new bond replaces the old one (one active per account). Status on the Trust page: missing, expired, under-covered against funds held, renewal due within 30 days, or ok.
- **Portal payments** (`portalPayments(residentId)` query, `portalPay(...)` mutation): scope `payments` (with two-step sign-in), audited as family access. Pays an issued statement with a saved method of that resident, through the same card path; a statement on a plan pays the next instalment, and a declined instalment sends the family to the business office. Another resident's statement or card reads as not found.
- **Database guards** (`20261113020000_phase_3b_money_guards`, hand-written, additive): one active plan per statement; plan and instalment amounts frozen, an instalment's payment changes only after a decline, instalments sum to the plan (deferred); schedule amounts frozen; one open application per stay; authorisation activation needs a different approver, one active per ledger; trust statements and interest runs frozen; interest run sums checked; one active bond per account. The generated `20261113015156_phase_3b_money` only creates the eight new tables (Prisma's spurious `DROP INDEX "PaymentAllocation_payment_statement_unique"` for the hand-made index was removed).
- **Tests**: `billing/ar.test.ts` and `trust/extras.test.ts` (fast-check: instalments sum, GL balances, interest shares sum); `mutations/phase3b.db.test.ts` (plan decline/retry/in-flight, portal pay scope/MFA/isolation/replay, GL export, Medicaid application and schedule, trust authorisation approval, interest, statements, alerts, bond). `trust.db.test.ts` now records and approves an authorisation before withdrawing.

### Deferred from 3b

- **GL account mapping per operator**: the export uses a fixed default chart (numbers printed on every line). A per-operator mapping table is a settings feature, not a money rule; deferred until an operator asks.
- **Portal UI**: the payments API is built and gated; the family portal screen for it belongs with the portal front end.
- **Quarterly statement delivery**: statements are made and downloadable as PDF; mailing or emailing them is notification machinery, which AGENTS.md rules out.
